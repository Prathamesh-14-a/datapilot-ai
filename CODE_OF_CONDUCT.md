# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to fostering a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

Our goal is to make DataPilot AI a collaborative and positive environment for learning, building, and contributing.

---

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Welcome constructive feedback.
- Help others learn and grow.
- Use inclusive language.
- Report issues responsibly.
- Focus on improving the project.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Offensive or abusive language
- Spam or malicious content
- Sharing confidential information
- Any illegal activity

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact:

**Email:** datapilotai.app@gmail.com

Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled appropriately.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to:

- Remove inappropriate comments or content
- Reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct
- Temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary
